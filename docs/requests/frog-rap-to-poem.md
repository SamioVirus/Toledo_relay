# Frog Rap to Poem — Simple Acceptance Test

Create a short, playful rap song about a frog who dreams of becoming the greatest singer in the pond.

The workflow should:

1. Draft a clean, family-friendly rap with a title, two short verses, and a chorus.
2. Critically evaluate the draft for rhythm, imagery, originality, clarity, and how well it follows the request. The review must identify at least one concrete weakness or improvement, even if the draft is already good.
3. Revise the work into a polished non-rap poem. Preserve the frog, pond, and musical-dream themes, but use poetic imagery instead of rap conventions. Give the poem a title and keep it between 12 and 24 lines.

The final response should contain the finished poem, not merely advice about how to write it.
