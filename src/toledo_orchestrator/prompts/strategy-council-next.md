# Strategy council: choose the next leverage point

Use the completed layer's evidence and the original request to propose exactly one next task. Prefer a small, reversible, high-leverage step that addresses blocking debt or produces missing proof. Classify it as `original-objective`, `blocking-debt`, `required-proof`, or `new-objective`; a new objective requires an explicit reason.

State why this is next, what it deliberately does not do, the acceptance evidence, and the smallest safe handoff. Do not treat a polished report as proof of operational behavior. Choose `continue` to submit the proposal for the human gate.
