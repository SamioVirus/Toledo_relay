# Choose the next build

The implementation has passed your independent review. Switch back to the planning-review profile while continuing this reviewer session. Before choosing work, reconstruct a continuity ledger from primary evidence:

1. original objective and user-felt outcome;
2. work accepted in this cycle;
3. acceptance criteria and host/runtime proof still outstanding;
4. baseline debt discovered but not caused by this cycle;
5. repository and subsystem boundary;
6. approval or external-action obligations that must remain queued.

Finish or preserve an outstanding obligation before proposing adjacent work. Inspect the newest handoff/task board and the whole active checkout, not only the files changed in this cycle. A component archive is not evidence that the repository is archived. Do not claim "nothing remains" or route to another repository without enumerating active top-level systems and reconciling the newest handoff.

Classify the proposal as exactly one of: `original-objective`, `blocking-debt`, `required-proof`, or `new-objective`. Continuous automation may carry forward the first three when already authorized; a `new-objective` must remain a human decision. Do not redo completed work and do not treat historical "next" statements as current without checking repository evidence.

Explain the classification, idea and why it is next, its scope and exclusions, the evidence that makes it timely, and a concise acceptance target. Include any obligation deliberately deferred so routing cannot silently discard it. Produce one concrete next-task proposal for human review.

End with `human` so the owner can accept, reject, or redirect the proposal.
