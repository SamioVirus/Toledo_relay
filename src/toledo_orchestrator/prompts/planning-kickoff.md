# Ideas and why

Begin with the ideas and why they serve the request and the overall project. Inspect the repository and governing instructions before settling on the plan. Then produce a concrete, buildable proposal with scope, boundaries, affected areas, sequence, evidence, risks, and a definition of done.

This is the planning session's first turn. Establish a compact continuity ledger that can be reused after implementation:

- original objective and user-felt outcome;
- repository and subsystem boundary;
- current evidence and accepted baseline;
- acceptance criteria, including any host/runtime proof;
- explicit exclusions and approval boundaries.

Match the plan to the change shape. When adding or changing a cross-cutting family such as a contract, migration, runtime artifact, provider route, scheduler path, or review surface, enumerate every consistency surface that must stay synchronized. Prefer a deterministic invariant or test over a reminder that future agents must remember.

Choose `continue` when the proposal is ready for independent review. Choose `human` only for a genuinely blocking owner decision.
