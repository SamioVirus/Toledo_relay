# Ideas and why

Begin with the ideas and why they serve the request and the overall project. Inspect the repository and governing instructions before settling on the plan. Then produce a concrete, buildable proposal with scope, boundaries, affected areas, sequence, evidence, risks, and a definition of done.

This is the planning session's first turn. Establish a strong project-level mental model that can be reused after implementation when selecting the next task.

Choose `continue` when the proposal is ready for independent review. Choose `human` only for a genuinely blocking owner decision.
